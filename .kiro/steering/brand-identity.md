# ELOOX Brand Identity

## Company

**Brand name:** ELOOX  
**Tagline:** BUILD SMARTER. SCALE FURTHER.  
**Brand expansion:**
- E – Elevate
- L – Lead
- O – Optimize
- O – Operate
- X – Xplore

ELOOX is an international technology and business growth company with two divisions:
1. **ELOOX Services** – technology, automation, software and revenue growth services.
2. **ELOOX Products** – proprietary SaaS products and software platforms.

## Target industries

- Education (schools, colleges, universities)
- Small and medium businesses
- Startups
- Corporate offices

## Tone of voice

- Confident and authoritative — never arrogant.
- Direct and concise — no filler or hype.
- International and professional — accessible globally.
- Results-oriented — speak in outcomes, not features.
- Never invent client testimonials, project results, or company credentials.

## Visual identity rules

### Typography
- **Headings (H1–H3):** Space Grotesk or Sora, bold weight.
- **Subheadings (H4–H6):** Space Grotesk or Sora, medium weight.
- **Body text:** Inter, regular weight, 16–18px base size.
- **UI labels / captions:** Inter, 12–14px.

### Colour palette
| Token | Value | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| `--color-bg` | `#FFFFFF` | Primary background |
| `--color-surface` | `#F5F5F5` | Card / section background |
| `--color-ink` | `#0A0A0A` | Primary text |
| `--color-ink-secondary` | `#5A5A5A` | Secondary text |
| `--color-accent` | `#0066FF` | Primary accent (CTA, links, highlights) |
| `--color-accent-dark` | `#0047B3` | Hover state for accent |
| `--color-bold-bg` | `#0A0A0A` | Bold black sections |
| `--color-bold-text` | `#FFFFFF` | Text on bold sections |
| `--color-border` | `#E5E5E5` | Borders and dividers |

### Layout principles
- Mostly white backgrounds with bold black sections for contrast.
- Bold editorial design — large type, strong hierarchy.
- Premium 3D technology elements (CSS transforms or Three.js / Spline embeds).
- Smooth animations (Framer Motion preferred).
- Asymmetrical layouts — avoid rigid symmetry.
- Generous whitespace.

### Imagery rules
- Use abstract technology visuals, geometric forms, and 3D renders.
- Avoid stock-photo clichés (handshakes, generic teams, etc.).
- Never use AI-generated fake people.
- Product screenshots must be real when available; use stylised mockups otherwise.

## Logo usage

- Always reproduce the wordmark in full capitals: **ELOOX**.
- Minimum clear space: equal to the cap-height of the X on all sides.
- Do not distort, recolour, or add effects to the logo.
